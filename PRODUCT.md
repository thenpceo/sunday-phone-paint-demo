# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors encountering Sunday through a desktop-led interactive campaign, with a phone available as the physical controller for the experience.

## Product Purpose

Turn a phone into a spray-paint tool that reveals a hidden Sunday memo on the desktop, then carry the visitor into a tactile 3D customization experience. Success feels immediate, surprising, and physical while the cross-device connection remains understandable.

## Brand Personality

Playful, precise, and experimental. The experience should feel like a polished Sunday artifact: clean enough to trust, strange enough to remember, and driven by motion rather than explanatory UI.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, tutorial-heavy calibration screens, marker-dominated tracking interfaces, cluttered corner pills, conventional scroll-section storytelling, and mouse-based substitutes for the phone interaction.

## Design Principles

- Make the phone feel like the tool, not a remote control panel.
- Preserve the artwork as the dominant interface.
- Keep cross-device feedback immediate and legible.
- Use motion to communicate state and physicality.
- Retain the Sunday navigation and identity across every transition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong contrast and readable labels, provide keyboard access to the customization controls, preserve semantic status updates, and replace nonessential movement with quick state changes when reduced motion is requested.
